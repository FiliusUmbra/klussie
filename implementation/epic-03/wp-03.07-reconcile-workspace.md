## Findings

**1 · Real coverage is thin, and the reconciliation says so rather than
hiding it.** Ten of thirteen tables hold zero rows on staging. A
reconciliation that reported a bare "PASSED" here would read identically
whether it had checked seven rows or seven thousand. The gate now prints
its own coverage and flags when it is thin — a discipline directly
descended from `RECONCILE_IDENTITY.sql`'s "refuses to pass when there is
nothing to compare," adapted rather than copied because the underlying
condition is different: identity's empty-database case was an
unseeded *environment*; this epic's case is unseeded *marketplace
fixtures* in an otherwise-populated one, already documented as a known
gap by WP 03.06.

## Notes

**This closes the loop the roadmap's risk register opened.** Risk 1
("Epic 03 backfill assigns rows to the wrong workspace") is the
highest-severity item in `IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP.md` §15, and its stated
mitigation is exactly this package: "03.07 reconciliation is a hard gate
on 03.09." That gate has now run and passed, over real data, with its
own coverage honestly reported alongside the result.

**What remains before WP 03.09/03.10/03.12 can safely rely on this at
scale:** real marketplace fixtures. Nothing in this package or WP 03.06
requires them to exist — both are correct and complete as written — but
a reconciliation over seven rows in one table group is a real pass over
thin evidence, not equivalent to one over a representative population.
